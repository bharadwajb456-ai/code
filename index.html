About

Healthcare should work better than it does today. Too often, it's complicated, expensive and difficult to navigate. At UnitedHealth Group, we're working to change that. Through UnitedHealthcare and Optum, we're helping make care more affordable, accessible and connected — so more people can live healthier lives and the healthcare system works better for everyone.

Simplifying healthcare

People are better able to focus on their health when care is easier to get, benefits are easier to understand and medicines are more affordable. UnitedHealth Group brings together Optum and UnitedHealthcare to help connect benefits, care, pharmacy and support around the people we serve.
